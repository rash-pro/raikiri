Good to know

Reconnect weekly. While the app's publishing status is Testing, Google expires the sign-in after 7 days and the YouTube card shows Not connected. Click Connect YouTube again. Publishing the app to production avoids this, but requires a homepage and privacy policy on a verified domain.
The stream-info dock sends YouTube the title and tags (plus the Gaming category). The specific game can't be set through the YouTube API.
